# Dominion: Beyond the Stars

A mobile-first, mechanics-first grand strategy space game.

## Current playable systems
- 900 procedurally generated star systems
- 32 civilizations total (player + 31 autonomous AI empires)
- Hyperlane exploration, surveying, claims, stations, colonies, population growth
- Credits, alloys, science, influence, technology progression
- Independent fleets with locations, hull integrity and ship composition
- Corvette, frigate and cruiser hull classes
- Fleet movement through hyperlanes
- Fleet power derived from ships, technology and damage
- War declarations and hostile fleet combat
- System conquest during wars
- AI fleet construction, expansion and wartime movement
- Bilateral relations, improving relations, rivalries, alliances and peace
- Persistent local autosave
- Phone-first interface

## Next systems
