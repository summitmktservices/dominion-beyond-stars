# Dominion: Beyond the Stars

A mobile-first, mechanics-first grand strategy space game.

## Current playable systems
- 900 procedurally generated star systems
- 32 civilizations total (player + 31 autonomous AI empires)
- Hyperlane exploration, surveying, claims, stations, colonies, population growth
- Credits, alloys, science, influence, technology progression
- Independent fleets with locations, hull integrity and ship composition
- Corvette, frigate and cruiser hull classes
- Fleet movement through hyperlanes
- Fleet power derived from ships, technology and damage
- War declarations and hostile fleet combat
- System conquest during wars
- AI fleet construction, expansion and wartime movement
- Bilateral relations, improving relations, rivalries, alliances and peace
- Persistent local autosave
- Phone-first interface

## Tradition mechanics integration
Traditions now feed a centralized empire modifier engine rather than acting as descriptive unlocks. Active nodes and completed trees directly modify production, research, unity, trade, influence, population growth, stability, crime, housing, construction costs, colony and claim costs, fleet costs and combat power, leader progression, diplomacy, war exhaustion and planetary recovery. AI empires use the same modifiers for economic development, expansion and fleet construction, allowing societal paths to create materially different simulation outcomes.


## Deep technology system
Research is now scaled to grand-strategy depth: 234 original technologies across Physics, Society and Engineering, organized into 18 research families and six tiers. Technologies use prerequisite chains, weighted randomized research cards, rare-tech weighting, tradition-driven extra alternatives and rare-tech access, escalating costs, completed-tech histories, and late-game repeatable research. Technology effects feed the same live modifier engine as traditions, altering economy, research, growth, diplomacy, construction, fleets, warfare and planetary development. AI civilizations draw from and complete the same technology system.


## Technology-gated capabilities and naval scale
Research now gates concrete capabilities rather than only percentage modifiers. Engineering depth unlocks nine military hull classes—Interceptor, Corvette, Frigate, Destroyer, Cruiser, Carrier, Battleship, Dreadnought and Titan—with increasing alloy cost and combat weight. The mobile Fleet screen now acts as a shipyard for constructing individual hulls into fleets, and AI fleet construction uses the same unlock progression. Research milestones also gate terraforming, advanced laboratories, automation facilities, planetary fortresses, genetics progression and grand-works infrastructure. Research cards surface their capability progression alongside numerical effects.


## Operational warfare layer
Military simulation now goes beyond fleet power totals. Fleets track ammunition, supply, morale, experience, embarked armies, command usage, doctrine and engagement stance. Six doctrines—line, screen, carrier, missile, raider and siege—interact with fleet composition, while kinetic, energy and missile loadouts interact with plating, shields and screening. Friendly territory replenishes supply/ammunition and repairs damaged fleets at an alloy cost; extended operations and command-cap overages reduce readiness. Fleet combat consumes ammunition and supply, damages morale, builds veteran experience, incorporates starbase defenses and logs post-battle condition. Players can refit fleets, recruit armies, bombard hostile worlds and launch ground invasions that can capture systems and colonies. AI fleets now move during wars, pursue hostile targets and trigger battles under the same operational rules. Save format v10.


## Strategic warfare expansion
Warfare now includes modular fleet refits, advanced weapon and armor unlocks, combat computers, auxiliary modules, admiral assignment and combat experience, emergency disengagement, connected-territory supply tracing, orbital fortification, claims, blockades/raiding, limited military intelligence and enforceable peace settlements. Fleet designs support kinetic, energy, missile, plasma, rail and lance weapons; plating, shields, balanced, reactive and ablative protection; brawler, artillery, picket, carrier and evasive computers; and repair, afterburner, jammer, targeting, magazine and supply auxiliaries, with advanced components gated by research. Admirals improve assigned fleets and gain XP from combat. Supply now requires a connected friendly path to the imperial core rather than merely standing in any friendly system, making territorial corridors strategically meaningful. Raider fleets can damage enemy trade and colony stability while partially living off hostile territory. Orbital defenses can be expanded with alloys. Claims can be established before or during war, and peace settlements can transfer occupied claimed systems once war exhaustion creates leverage. Save format v11.


## Exploration and frontier expansion
The galaxy layer now uses empire-specific knowledge instead of universal map information. Each civilization begins knowing only its home region and receives a dedicated science vessel with an assigned scientist. Science ships reveal nearby systems through sensors, execute multi-turn surveys, gain scientist experience and can auto-explore. Survey duration improves with scientific advancement and exploration traditions. Systems can contain original anomaly encounters that are revealed by surveying and resolved for research, influence, alloys or unity. Civilizations encountered in space begin as unknown contacts; bilateral first-contact projects progress over time, and foreign empires remain hidden from the diplomacy interface until contact completes. Expansion now requires surveyed territory and connected frontier access: influence is spent to construct an outpost, the outpost establishes ownership/borders and sensor visibility, and colonies can only be founded on surveyed habitable worlds inside owned space. AI civilizations use the same science-ship surveying, auto-exploration, outpost and colonization constraints. Save format v12.
